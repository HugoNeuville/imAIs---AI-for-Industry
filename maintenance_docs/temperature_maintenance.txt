Temperature Monitoring Maintenance Procedure

High temperature may indicate overheating, insufficient cooling, excessive operational load, airflow obstruction, or lubrication degradation.

Potential causes:
- Cooling system malfunction
- Insufficient airflow
- Excessive machine load
- Lubrication degradation
- Friction increase in rotating components

Recommended inspection steps:
1. Inspect cooling system and airflow.
2. Verify fan operation and ventilation conditions.
3. Check lubrication levels and lubrication quality.
4. Review recent operating load conditions.
5. Inspect rotating components for excessive friction.
6. Compare current temperature with historical baseline.

Recommended action:
- If temperature remains elevated, schedule maintenance inspection within 24 hours.
- If temperature reaches critical levels, stop machine and inspect immediately.
