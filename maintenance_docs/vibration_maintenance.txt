Vibration Monitoring Maintenance Procedure

High vibration may indicate bearing wear, shaft misalignment, imbalance, loosened components, or rotating equipment degradation.

Potential causes:
- Bearing degradation
- Shaft misalignment
- Rotor imbalance
- Mechanical looseness
- Rotating component wear

Recommended inspection steps:
1. Inspect bearings for wear or overheating.
2. Verify shaft alignment.
3. Inspect rotating components and fixation points.
4. Check for mechanical looseness or imbalance.
5. Compare vibration levels with historical trends.
6. Review recent maintenance operations.

Recommended action:
- If vibration increases progressively, schedule preventive maintenance.
- If vibration becomes critical, stop equipment and inspect immediately.
