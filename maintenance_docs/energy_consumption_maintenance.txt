Energy Consumption Monitoring Maintenance Procedure

High energy consumption may indicate abnormal load, motor stress, inefficient operating mode, excessive friction, or mechanical resistance.

Potential causes:
- Motor overload
- Mechanical resistance
- Friction increase
- Inefficient operating mode
- Abnormal production conditions

Recommended inspection steps:
1. Review recent operating load conditions.
2. Inspect motor temperature and vibration.
3. Verify rotating component alignment.
4. Check for excessive friction or resistance.
5. Compare energy consumption with historical baseline.
6. Review production operating conditions.

Recommended action:
- If energy consumption remains elevated, schedule maintenance review within 24 hours.
- If energy consumption becomes critical, inspect motor system immediately.
